# Logo concept images

The 12 full logo concepts shown on `/brand` (Logo concepts section). Each
concept has two files: the full 1200×1200 image and a 600-wide thumbnail
used in the comparison grid. Catalogue/metadata for these lives in
`src/lib/logo-concepts.ts`.

| # | Name | File (full) | File (thumb) | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Seal | `concept-01-seal-sun-strata.webp` | `concept-01-seal-sun-strata-600.webp` | A circular seal, sun over layered strata. |
| 02 | Rings | `concept-02-interlocking-rings.webp` | `concept-02-interlocking-rings-600.webp` | Two linked rings, one generation and the next. |
| 03 | Hourglass | `concept-03-hourglass-strata.webp` | `concept-03-hourglass-strata-600.webp` | Time passing, layered into the lower half. |
| 04 | Growth rings | `concept-04-growth-rings.webp` | `concept-04-growth-rings-600.webp` | Concentric arcs, open at the top; time accumulating. |
| 05 | Stepped column | `concept-05-stepped-column.webp` | `concept-05-stepped-column-600.webp` | Three blocks stepping forward under a lintel. |
| 06 | Bridge | `concept-06-bridge-arch.webp` | `concept-06-bridge-arch-600.webp` | An arch connecting two shores. |
| 07 | Monogram seal | `concept-07-monogram-seal.webp` | `concept-07-monogram-seal-600.webp` | I J F as a stamp, ruled above and below. |
| 08 | Ledger | `concept-08-open-ledger.webp` | `concept-08-open-ledger-600.webp` | An open book with a horizon across the pages. |
| 09 | Compass | `concept-09-compass-star.webp` | `concept-09-compass-star-600.webp` | An eight-point star, north elongated. |

| 12 | Wordmark | `concept-12-wordmark-horizon.webp` | `concept-12-wordmark-horizon-600.webp` | Type-led; the g's descender becomes a horizon. |

All paths above are relative to this folder: `public/brand/logos/`. In the
browser they're served at `/brand/logos/<file>` (anything under `public/`
is served from the site root as-is).

These are references to react to, not finished artwork — whichever concept
gets chosen is redrawn as clean vector and recoloured for the chosen brand
direction before it ships for real.
